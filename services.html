Our expertise

Full-service mechanical contracting

ZAR Mechanical offers complete mechanical contracting for commercial and industrial construction projects.

HVAC systems

Installation, retrofits, and replacements of air handling units, rooftop units (RTUs), boilers, and chillers. We plan the work around occupied buildings so comfort stays online.

Plumbing systems

Complete plumbing installations, including domestic water, sanitary, storm, and natural gas piping — coordinated with the rest of the mechanical scope.

Sheet metal & ductwork

Fabrication and installation of duct systems designed for efficiency and performance, from gymnasium AHUs to tight commercial risers.

Design-build services

Integrated design and construction solutions tailored to your needs. One team owns the drawings, the install, and the outcome.

Maintenance & service

Preventive maintenance programs and service to protect your investment, with round-the-clock coverage when something can’t wait.

Why it matters

One roof. Fewer surprises.

Comprehensive mechanical expertise means seamless coordination, reduced downtime, and efficient project execution from start to finish.
