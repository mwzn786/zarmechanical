ZAR Mechanical Inc.

ZAR Mechanical Inc. is a Canadian-owned and operated mechanical contracting company founded by industry professionals with over 15 years of experience in commercial and industrial construction.

Our certified plumbers and HVAC technicians bring technical expertise, hands-on experience, and a commitment to doing things right the first time. Whether it’s a school retrofit, a new commercial build, or an industrial facility upgrade, we provide reliable solutions that last.
